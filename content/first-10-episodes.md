# First 10 TikTok Quiz Episodes

Format rule for every episode:
- 4 regular games + 1 BOSS.
- Four **visual** clues per game.
- No words baked into clue images.
- Prefer original AI-generated/generic object artwork instead of screenshots, logos or copyrighted promotional art.
- Difficulty should rise every level.
- One clue appears roughly every second, then a short thinking pause, then the answer.
